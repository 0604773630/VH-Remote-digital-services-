Company Registration

CIPC Registration — We File For You

No back-and-forth guidance. You give us your documents and requirements, we handle the entire CIPC filing process on your behalf — including companies with international directors.

How It Works
01
You Send Your Requirements
Tell us the company name, structure, and director details. We confirm what documents we need from you via WhatsApp or email.
02
You Supply the Documents
Send us certified IDs, passport copies for international directors, proof of address, and any other required documentation.
03
We Build & File with CIPC
We prepare and submit your complete registration application directly with CIPC. You don't need to touch the CIPC system at all.
04
You Receive Your Certificate
Once approved by CIPC, we deliver your company registration certificate and documents digitally — straight to your inbox.

Documents We Typically Need

  • ✓
    Certified copy of SA ID or passport (all directors)
  • ✓
    Proof of residential address (not older than 3 months)
  • ✓
    Proposed company name (we check availability)
  • ✓
    Company type (Pty Ltd, NPC, etc.)
  • ✓
    Director shareholding split (if multiple directors)
  • ✓
    Business activity description
  • ✓
    Contact details of all directors
🌍 International directors accepted

Companies with directors holding foreign citizenship or passports are fully supported. Additional documentation may apply depending on the director's country of origin.
